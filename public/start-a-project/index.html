Start a Project#

This written brief replaces the discovery call.

We don’t do 45-minute introductory Zoom meetings. The questions below collect only the information genuinely required to evaluate, scope, and build your website with precision.

Payment & Terms#

  • 50% deposit upfront upon project acceptance to reserve your build slot and begin construction.
  • 50% balance before final handover and live domain launch.
  • One revision round included per package. Additional revision rounds or out-of-scope requests are quoted separately.

Project Intake Brief#

Please review the fields below. You can fill them out and send them directly via email to loopwright@agentmail.to or submit using the brief generator below.

1. Identity & Contact#

  • Your Name:
  • Your Email:
  • Your Creative Practice: (e.g. Author, Novelist, Visual Artist, Independent Publisher, Studio)

2. Scope & Tier#

  • Selected Package:
    • [ ] SITE (€950) — Up to 5 pages, responsive, SEO, custom domain
    • [ ] MIGRATION (€1,450) — Migration from Wix/Squarespace/Webflow with URL preservation
    • [ ] PUBLISHER (€1,950) — Website + publication engine + newsletter integration
    • [ ] CARE (€40/month) — Ongoing uptime care & monthly content maintenance
  • Required Pages: (e.g. Home, Books/Work, About, Essays/Selected Writings, Contact)
  • Existing Website (if any): (Include current URL)

3. Content & Assets#

  • Is your content ready?
    • [ ] Yes, all text and images are ready to deliver
    • [ ] Partially ready (drafting underway)
    • [ ] Need guidance on content structure
  • Newsletter Requirements (if applicable): (Provider name, e.g. Buttondown, Substack, MailerLite, or none)

4. Aesthetics & Taste#

  • Visual References: (2–3 websites you admire for their layout, typography, or atmosphere)
  • Dislikes: (Elements you specifically want to avoid, e.g. popups, corporate sans-serif, animations)
  • Tone/Atmosphere: (e.g. classical serif, austere ledger, minimal dark gallery, warm paper)

5. Domain & Timeline#

  • Target Custom Domain: (e.g. yourname.com — do you already own it, and where is it registered?)
  • Ideal Launch Date / Deadline: (e.g. ahead of book release date, exhibition, or within 3–4 weeks)
