Open Source & Ventures

Open Source, Products & Ventures#

Craft developed in private. Useful tools documented and released open.

infinave operates across several interconnected creative and engineering workshops. When we build infrastructure for ourselves or solve complex editorial problems, we release reusable patterns as open-source software and package polished systems as standalone digital products.

Open-Source Tools#

We believe in durable, zero-dependency tools that put creators in control of their own computing:

  • Clean Static Publishing: Micro-utilities for static document pipelines, markdown ledger validation, and privacy-first site deployment.
  • Privacy Defaults: Boilerplates that eliminate third-party telemetry, font surveillance, and cookie rot.
  • Code Repositories: Explore our public code on GitHub. (Repository naming transition staged for review).

Digital Products#

For creators who prefer to build independently without hiring a studio:

  • Folio — The Editorial Author Template
    A distinguished, zero-dependency HTML5/CSS website template for novelists, poets, and essayists. Includes dedicated bibliography, excerpt anchors, agency representation notices, and light/dark mode support. Designed for free hosting on Cloudflare Pages or GitHub Pages. (Recommended: €49 · Staged for release).
  • Templates & Toolkits on Gumroad: Available on our Gumroad store.

Letter of Marque Games#

Letter of Marque Games is infinave’s dedicated venture label for independent games and interactive play.

  • Developed under the same philosophy of focused craftsmanship, high aesthetic discipline, and player respect.
  • Squabble: An original fast-paced word game, currently undergoing mobile architecture development toward a dedicated Android release under the Letter of Marque imprint.
