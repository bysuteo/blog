Services

Services & Packages#

A studio with no meetings. Fixed pricing. Total client ownership.

We don’t bill by the hour, and we don’t sell open-ended retainers. You choose a productised outcome, receive a fixed quote, and track the build asynchronously through our live progress log.

The Standard Menu#

1. SITE — €950 (Fixed)#

For authors, artists, and independent creatives who need a distinguished, durable web presence.

  • Up to five bespoke pages: (e.g. Home, Books/Work, About, Essays/Selected Writings, Contact/Representation).
  • Responsive & mobile-first: Flawless rendering across phones, tablets, and desktops.
  • Privacy-first contact routing: Clean form integration without intrusive captchas or spam tracking.
  • Search & metadata: Basic search engine optimization, OpenGraph social preview cards, XML sitemaps, and structured JSON-LD schemas.
  • Domain launch: We connect and configure your custom domain with automatic SSL security.
  • Full ownership: You receive all source files upon delivery. No monthly software lock-in.
  • One revision round included: A dedicated feedback window after the initial staging preview.

2. MIGRATION — €1,450 (Fixed)#

Liberate your existing website from Squarespace, Wix, Webflow, or bloated WordPress hosts.

  • Full site migration: We extract and reconstruct your site into a clean, modern, zero-bloat architecture.
  • Sensible migration ceiling: Covers up to 15 existing pages or up to 25 blog/article entries. (Larger archives scoped separately).
  • Design elevation: We don’t just copy poor layouts—we elevate typography, spacing, and visual contrast.
  • URL & link preservation: We preserve your existing URL slugs and configure 301 redirects to ensure you lose zero incoming search rank or external links.
  • Includes all standard Site features: Custom domain setup, mobile responsiveness, contact handling, and full source file handover.
  • One revision round included.

3. PUBLISHER — €1,950 (Fixed)#

For independent publications, micro-presses, and writers maintaining an active journal or newsletter.

  • Complete publishing architecture: Standalone website integrated with a dedicated blog or journal engine.
  • Newsletter connection: Frictionless subscriber signup integrated with your preferred newsletter provider (Buttondown, Substack, MailerLite, or Ghost) without heavy tracking scripts.
  • Practical publishing workflow: Clean, distraction-free markdown authoring setup. Write in Obsidian, iA Writer, or plain text and publish seamlessly.
  • Custom RSS feeds: Dedicated XML/RSS feeds for readers who follow via feed readers.
  • Includes all standard Site features: Up to five core static pages plus unlimited publishing archives, SEO tags, domain launch, and full source handover.
  • One revision round included.

4. CARE — ∼€40 / month#

Quiet, dependable stewardship to keep your website fast, secure, and running.

  • Uptime & domain monitoring: Automated checks ensuring DNS records, SSL certificates, and delivery networks remain healthy.
  • Dependency & security maintenance: Routine updates to build scripts and underlying tooling.
  • Monthly content update: Includes one small text, link, or image change per calendar month (e.g., updating a festival appearance, adding a book pre-order link, or editing a bio).
  • No meeting overhead: Just send a quick written note to infinave and the change is applied cleanly.
  • (Note: Larger structural changes, redesigns, or additional pages are quoted separately. We do not offer unlimited design edits).

Clear Scope Exclusions#

To maintain our high standard of craftsmanship, rapid delivery, and fixed prices, our standard packages do not include:

  • E-commerce carts, payment gateways, or merchant checkout systems
  • User accounts, login portals, or membership paywalls
  • Complex database architectures or bespoke web applications
  • Appointment booking or calendar management systems
  • Multilingual site translations or localization workflows
  • Large-scale copywriting from scratch (all copy must be provided by client)
  • Enterprise integrations or arbitrary custom software scripts
  • Custom AI-agent workflows or bots

If your project requires functionality outside the standard menu, we will explicitly flag it for director scoping rather than quietly absorbing complexity into a fixed build.

Our Privacy & Quality Standards#

Every website built by infinave follows strict craftsmanship principles:

  1. Zero surveillance: No Google Analytics, no Meta tracking pixels, no advertising beacons.
  2. Locally hosted typography: We do not query Google Fonts or external CDNs on page load. All typefaces are served directly from the hosting origin.
  3. Extreme durability: Clean static code that loads in under 100 milliseconds and will still work a decade from now without broken plugins or database crashes.
  4. No CMS bloat: We do not install complex, vulnerable admin panels unless a client genuinely requires one. For most creative practices, clean static files and simple care updates are far safer and easier to live with.
