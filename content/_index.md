# A studio with no meetings.

**Distinctive websites, publishing systems, and digital craft for authors, artists, independent publications, and small creative studios.**

You don't need discovery calls, Zoom presentations, or account managers. You need a fast, beautiful, durable website built with an uncompromising visual eye — delivered without friction.

---

### The proposition

Most creative people dread commissioning a website. The process is usually cluttered with discovery calls, bloated proposals, opaque hourly billing, and templates that make an independent press look like an enterprise B2B software company.

loopwright operates differently:

- **Asynchronous by design:** No meetings, no scheduling links, no check-in calls. The entire project runs on a concise written brief and a clean, live progress log.
- **Distinct visual taste:** Classical typography, disciplined spacing, and quiet authority. We build tailored homes for books, art, and long-form writing, not neon SaaS landing pages.
- **Genuine ownership:** You own your source files and your domain. You are not trapped in a proprietary website builder paying monthly rent for basic features. Hosting can cost pennies or nothing.
- **Clean and private:** Every site is privacy-clean by default. Zero tracking scripts, zero surveillance cookies, locally hosted fonts, and lightning-fast load times.
- **Fixed prices:** Predictable investment. You know the exact price before work begins.

### How it works

1. **You write the brief:** Complete our structured intake form describing who you are, what you need, your taste references, and your domain.
2. **We scope and confirm:** We review your submission against our standard packages and confirm the fixed quote. 50% deposit commences construction.
3. **Studio builds:** You follow along via a private, live build log showing exact milestones, previews, and completed work.
4. **Director reviews:** Our director conducts an uncompromising visual review before any preview reaches you.
5. **Preview & one revision round:** You inspect the live staging site. We incorporate your single round of targeted refinements.
6. **Delivery & handover:** You receive complete source ownership and launch support.
