lux keeps a contemplative notebook. The writing comes in five forms (meditations, offices, signs, icons and examens), and the site gives each its own section rather than pouring them into one stream of posts.

The design is built for slow reading: a measured column, text-size controls on every piece, light and dark modes, and no pop-ups or tracking.
