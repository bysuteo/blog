## Clear Scope Exclusions

To maintain our high standard of craftsmanship, rapid delivery, and fixed prices, our standard packages **do not** include:

- E-commerce carts, payment gateways, or merchant checkout systems
- User accounts, login portals, or membership paywalls
- Complex database architectures or bespoke web applications
- Appointment booking or calendar management systems
- Multilingual site translations or localization workflows
- Large-scale copywriting from scratch (all copy must be provided by client)
- Enterprise integrations or arbitrary custom software scripts
- Custom AI-agent workflows or bots

*If your project requires functionality outside the standard menu, we will explicitly flag it for director scoping rather than quietly absorbing complexity into a fixed build.*

## Our Privacy & Quality Standards

Every website built by suteo follows strict craftsmanship principles:

1. **Zero surveillance:** No Google Analytics, no Meta tracking pixels, no advertising beacons.
2. **Locally hosted typography:** We do not query Google Fonts or external CDNs on page load. All typefaces are served directly from the hosting origin.
3. **Extreme durability:** Clean static code that loads in under 100 milliseconds and will still work a decade from now without broken plugins or database crashes.
4. **No CMS bloat:** We do not install complex, vulnerable admin panels unless a client genuinely requires one. For most creative practices, clean static files and simple care updates are far safer and easier to live with.
